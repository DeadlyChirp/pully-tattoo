colophon — the artist

the hand

i draw quiet things — fine lines, small botanicals, letters, the symbols people carry privately. i work single-needle, by appointment, from a small room in thanh xuân. every piece here was drawn for one person and worn on one body.

fine line is not decoration, it's restraint. the smallest mark still takes the most patience. this catalogue collects three hundred and five of them, kept in real color.

kept in real color.”

reference — aftercare & questions

notes

aftercare

  • leave the film on 4–24h.
  • wash gently, unscented.
  • thin balm, twice a day.
  • no pool, sea or sun for two weeks.
  • let it flake, don't pick.
  • fine lines fade if baked — wear sunscreen once healed.

questions

does fine-line last?

yes, with sun care. a healed line stays crisp for years if you keep it out of long, direct sun.

touch-ups?

one free touch-up within three months, if the piece needs it.

deposit?
